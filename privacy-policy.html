◐ Auzu Alarm Privacy Policy Effective Date: August 17, 2026 1. Introduction Auzu Alarm ("Auzu Alarm", "we", "us", or "our") is a waking alarm application designed to help users wake up and dismiss an alarm through voice verification. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, where it is processed, how it is protected, and how users can delete or reset their stored voice enrollment data. Privacy by design: Auzu Alarm is designed so that voice verification can be performed locally on the user's Android device. The core alarm-verification feature does not require uploading the user's voice recordings to a remote server. 2. Information We Access Microphone and voice data Auzu Alarm requests microphone access because voice is an essential part of its alarm-dismissal feature. During initial setup, the user records voice samples for enrollment. When an alarm is ringing, the app may access the microphone to capture the user's spoken recitation and compare it with the enrolled voice profile. Voice enrollment information The app may create and store a local voice profile or other derived representation needed to perform voice matching. This information is used solely for the Auzu Alarm voice-verification feature. Alarm and app settings The app may store information necessary to operate alarms, such as alarm times, enabled or disabled status, alarm preferences, and local application settings. Device information Auzu Alarm may process limited technical information required for normal Android operation, such as application state, audio input status, and operating-system capabilities. We do not use this information to build advertising profiles. 3. How Voice Data Is Used Creating the user's voice enrollment/profile during setup. Checking whether a spoken recitation matches the enrolled voice profile. Allowing the user to dismiss the alarm after the required verification. Supporting reliable local verification on the user's device. Auzu Alarm does not use voice data for advertising, profiling users, selling information, or unrelated purposes. 4. Local and Offline Processing The core voice-matching process is designed to operate locally on the user's Android device. Voice data used for verification is not intentionally transmitted to Auzu Alarm servers as part of the core alarm feature. If a future version changes how voice data is transmitted, stored, or processed, this Privacy Policy will be updated and relevant in-app disclosures and permissions will be provided where required. 5. Storage and Security Where a local voice profile is stored, Auzu Alarm is designed to protect it using Android security mechanisms, including Android Keystore-backed protection where supported by the implementation and device. We use appropriate technical safeguards intended to protect locally stored information against unauthorized access. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. 6. Voice Recordings and Retention Voice information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide voice-verification functionality. If the implementation stores a derived voice profile rather than the original recording, the original enrollment recording should not be retained after enrollment unless the app explicitly tells the user otherwise. Users can reset or remove their voice enrollment through the application's available reset/delete functionality. Uninstalling the application may also remove locally stored application data according to Android's normal app-data behavior. If the app provides a "Delete Voice Profile" or "Reset Voice Enrollment" option, that option should permanently remove the locally stored enrollment information. 7. Sharing of Information Auzu Alarm does not sell, rent, or share the user's voice enrollment data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties for advertising or profiling. The core voice-verification feature is intended to operate locally and does not require sending voice recordings to a remote service. If third-party libraries or services are added and they access personal or sensitive user data, the application's privacy disclosures and Google Play Data Safety information will be updated accordingly. 8. Permissions Microphone: required for voice enrollment and voice verification. Notifications: may be required to notify the user about an active alarm. Alarm-related Android capabilities: used to schedule and trigger alarms at times selected by the user. Permissions are used only for application functionality. Users can manage permissions through Android settings, although disabling a required permission may prevent the associated feature from working. 9. Children's Privacy Auzu Alarm is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling. If you believe a child has provided personal information in a manner inconsistent with this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the contact mechanism below. 10. Data Deletion and User Choices Users may reset their voice enrollment and delete locally stored application data using controls provided by the app and Android operating system. If you need assistance with deletion of information associated with Auzu Alarm, you may contact us using the contact mechanism below. Because the core voice profile is designed to be stored locally, there may be no corresponding copy of the voice profile on our servers. 11. Third-Party Services Auzu Alarm may use Android platform services and third-party software libraries necessary to provide or maintain app functionality. Such services may process technical information according to their own policies. We do not intentionally provide a user's voice enrollment data to third-party services for advertising or unrelated purposes. Any future service that materially changes the handling of personal or sensitive data will be reviewed and disclosed as required. 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's functionality, data practices, legal requirements, or security practices change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date. 13.
